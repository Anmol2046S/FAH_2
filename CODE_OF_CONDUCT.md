# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards others

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, offensive comments
- Trolling, insulting comments
- Sexual harassment
- Publishing private information
- Other unprofessional conduct

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by contacting conduct@example.com.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
